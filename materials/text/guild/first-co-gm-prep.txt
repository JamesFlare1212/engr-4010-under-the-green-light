Return to Headquarters – An Encounter
Your Guild is returning to headquarters after the conclusion and results of the All- Guild Meeting. Along the way, you find that the road is being blocked by a monster/creature native to the area where your headquarters is located on Heliora Prime.

As Co-GMs, you will need to utilize this prep worksheet to develop narration to set the scene for your Guild and guide your other Guild members on how they want to encounter the monster. Your Guild has at least three ways to encounter the monster – fight, understand/befriend, and/or try to escape, which the monster and terrain will not make easy.

Choices in how your Guild wants to encounter the monster will result in Guild members attempting to take actions and rolling the die approximately 2-4 times during the 20 minute one scene gameplay. Use the Rules & Mechanics sheet to determine possible bonuses and the success of the actions and potential results of the encounter. Reminder to lead with creativity and keep the options open to your Guild mates’ creativity – Yes, and.....

Remember to be affirming, supportive, and collaborative!

Monster/Creature Encounter Prep

On the way back to our Guild Headquarters in the Luminis Sea region of Heliora Prime

Our Guild encounters a /creature native to that region …….A Maid-Mer

The Maid-Mer looks like an inverted mermaid, with human legs and a fish’s body above the waist. Not just any fish though; the fish part alone is about the size of a Fiat 500, and the human legs are as tall as Jacob himself.

The monster/creature appears to be......severly hungry ( and hangry because of this) it is exhausted from catching his new food, so it really just wants to be alone right now. It is also bothered that his favorite viewpoint of the sea has been taken by a huge glass turtle (our HQ)

The Maid-Mer  is currently devouring handfuls of seagulls from a pile next to it. The seagulls in the area are notoriously evil, aggressive, and hard to catch….. (how fast or smart is this creature??)

The monster/creature is blocking the way for our Guild to continue to Headquarters. How do we want to proceed? What should we do next? Should we fight it, befriend it, or run?… (pick one way)

Concluding the Encounter
By the end of the 20-25 minutes Encounter game play, your Guild needs to end up back at headquarters. This can happen in different ways within different consequences and/or positive/negative results for certain Guild members or the whole Guild depending on what actions your Guild chooses to take in the encounter with the monster/creature. However, the whole Guild must have returned to headquarters at the conclusion of the scene. Please be sure to conclude the encounter effectively for your Guild.

All-Class Activity Preparation
After our one scene encounter game play, we will be engaging in an all-class role playing game activity in our Guilds focused on Cultural Competency topics. Your main role as a Co-GM will be to guide your Guild effectively through the activity and needed deliverables, take notes of discussion points in your Guild, and help decision making processes as needed.

To be effectively prepared for this portion of class, please be sure you complete the following content review on LMS for Week 6:

DICE ROLLS:

FIghting: 2-Dice Rolls

Roll 1- Defense
Success: You dodge its clumsy, powerful kick, and stay on your feet.
Failure: A deadly kick or hurled bird carcass hits the party; take a temporary combat penalty like a broken leg in the group.

Roll 2- Attack
Success: You knock the Maid-Mer off balance, forcing it to retreat down the beach in panic
Failure: It plants its giant legs and delivers a massive kick, forcing you to use emergency equipment to heal the group  to push through.

The guild pushes through to make it back to the Shell on time.
Lingering Effects: The injured Maidmer remains hostile around the cliffs, periodically disrupting water power sources. Healing the group members may take time and lots of resources if injuries did occur.

Befriending: 2-Rolls

Roll 1- Empathy
Success: You realize it isn't inherently evil—it’s just severely malnourished and stressed by losing its favorite coastal lookout spot.
Failure: You misinterpret its posture as an attack threat, causing it to grow defensive and raise its guard.


Roll 2- Persuasion
Success: You share supplies or promise a clearing on top of HQ's outer shell. It happily accepts, munches on the food, and steps aside.
Failure: The food offering isn't quite enough; it demands an immediate escort to a new location with a view.

The Guild returns safely to HQ together.
Lingering Consequence: The Maid-mer becomes a loyal, territorial perimeter guard, like a dog, around the glass dome of The Shell, keeping wild predators away in exchange for occasional food leftovers. It eats the evil seagulls around the base.

Avoiding: 2 Rolls:

Roll 1- Stealth
success: You draw its attention away toward the tree line without alerting it to your exact location.
Failure: You step on a dry branch or rock which the Maid-Mer hears because it has Echolocation., causing the Maid-Mer to whip its head around and block the bypass route.

Roll 2- Athletics
Success: The entire Guild safely scrambles over the high cliff overhang and down into the HQ entry yard.
Failure: A member slips on wet algae hitting their funny bone, requiring team assistance and forcing a noisy, chaotic dash into HQ doors.

The whole Guild makes it back inside HQ before the scene ends.
Lingering Consequence: The path back to HQ remains blocked by the grumpy Maidmer, complicating future land-based supply runs until dealt with later.
