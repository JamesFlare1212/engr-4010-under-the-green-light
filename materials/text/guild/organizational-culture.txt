Organizational Culture Guild Reflection Worksheet

What does your Guild value? Why?
Friendship – because friends are cool
Loyalty - we need to be able to stick up for one another and have each other’s backs
Communication- Very important to communicate properly in a social world
Wealth - because money
Humility - we need to be humble so that no one thinks they are better than the other so we can work together
Strength - Without strength it’s hard to earn respect and trust from other guilds.
Cooperation - We need to cooperate to get good work done
Personal Development- We can always be better and should push ourselves everyday, no one is perfect
Openness - Honesty and understanding within a group

What are the artifacts of your Guild – examples of physical representations of your cultural identity?
The turtle - the shell symbolizes how we protect the other soft groups
Water - fluidity, the ability to change when facing a new challenge
