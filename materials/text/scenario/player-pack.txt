UNDER THE GREEN LIGHT
PLAYER BRIEFING  /  GUARDIANS GUILD
The Shell, Luminis Sea, Heliora Prime
The situation
The coastal pump station is flooding. Twelve maintenance workers are inside. Your screen says GREEN — SAFE TO OPERATE. A storm surge will arrive in twelve fictional minutes. Once your plan is ready, you have three action windows. Discussion time does not secretly consume those windows.
Your three objectives
OPEN A ROUTE. Build or secure a usable rescue path.
MOVE THE PEOPLE. Account for twelve workers; two need assisted movement.
ISOLATE THE HAZARD. Verify the local shutdown and medical power handover.
Known safeguards and equipment
An upper refuge can hold four people for roughly two hours if evacuation is incomplete. The outside rescue crew arrives after the surge eases. The clinic has independent backup for twenty minutes. The station and refuge safeguards are separate. Shutdown affects noncritical Shell labs until the next day; it does not instantly cut clinic power.
Available: safety lines, harnesses, a hand winch, an eight-person-rated workboat, two NPC boat crew, and an exterior manual gate. The boat has six evacuee seats after its two crew; two trips move twelve people. You also have TWO shared emergency kits.
Choices you can propose
HIGH LINE: cliff rescue. Known cost: leave the heavy spare motor below the flood line for later repair or replacement.
QUIET WATER: two boat trips; secure the motor on its high rack from the sea-side hatch. Known cost: replace the spent lab battery pack and leave low-level tool crates behind. Clinic backup stays separate.
BORROWED CHANNEL: divert the water, then use the walkway. Known cost: disturb part of a nursery shallows for a season; commit to restoration and clean the motor.
YOUR PLAN: combine or replace these approaches. Explain access, people, isolation, and the assumptions your plan depends on.
This draft’s session conventions
D20 + one justified bonus: Leadership +3 OR Engineering +2 OR Tool +1. Preparation or a concrete creative solution may lower the relevant execution DC by up to 2; these reductions do not stack. Base execution DCs: route 12, people 12, isolation 13. Teacher instructions take priority.
After seeing an execution result, the group may spend one emergency kit to improve it by one result tier, at most once per roll and never above ordinary success. There are no extra natural-1 penalties. Say what you try; the GMs explain the result.

STAKEHOLDER PROMPTS
Optional: three players can voice these concerns during discussion, then return to their own characters. No secret objectives or player-versus-player rewards.
MIRA / BUILDERS
You approved the temporary bypass. The low-load test passed; the full-surge test was not completed. You want the workers safe, but you also fear losing the spare motor and everyone’s confidence.
Opening: “Could we keep the green status while we work? I am afraid the other guilds will stop trusting our equipment.”
You can offer structural knowledge and practical help. If the group asks for honest limits and a correction, cooperate. You do not withhold rescue help when they refuse an unrestricted safety statement.
Question for the group: “What exactly can we certify, and who verifies it?”

TAVI / LINKERS
You have an open communication channel. Other guilds need usable instructions, not certainty that the evidence cannot support.
Opening: “Give me what we know, what we do not know, and what people should do now. I can update the message later.”
You can transmit the location, hazards, headcount, restrictions and next update. You preserve the original message and record later corrections.
Question for the group: “Who needs this information, and when will we update them?”
________________________________________________________________
DR. VALE / LIFE WEAVERS
Two workers need assistance. You have independent clinic backup for twenty minutes. You need a realistic arrival plan and a named power-handover contact.
Opening: “Tell me who needs assistance, who is bringing them, and who will confirm my next power source.”
You help once the group gives workable arrangements. You do not rank lives by status or make rescue conditional on a persuasion roll.
Question for the group: “Whose needs are missing from our plan?”

EVERYONE HAS A JOB
Six temporary focus roles. Keep your own character; choose or swap a focus. No focus grants extra numerical bonuses or exclusive access.
1 / STRUCTURES & ACCESS
Ask what supports the route. Explain a check of anchors, walkway, mooring or barriers. Lead one preparation or execution action.
2 / WATER & ENVIRONMENT
Observe the tide, calm water and nursery shallows. Identify a route cost and a way to reduce harm.
3 / PEOPLE & CARE
Plan assisted movement and a headcount. Ask who receives the workers and checks the medical handover.
4 / POWER & TOOLS
Distinguish a screen claim from verified local state. Consult the appropriate expert before confirming isolation.
5 / COMMUNICATION & TRUST
Draft a short factual message. Invite one concern the group has not heard. Name an owner and a next update.
6 / COORDINATION & CHALLENGE
Summarize the plan, capacity limits and fallback. Ask one respectful “what if?” before the group commits.
